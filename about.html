OUR STORY

A country church with open arms.

Cowboy Church of Maple Valley is a non-denominational, Bible-based church where everyone is welcome.

Founded with willing hearts

Cowboy Church of Maple Valley was founded by Bruce and Heather Deur. For many years, Bruce faithfully shared God's Word with friends, while Heather serves by teaching Sunday school and encouraging families in their faith.

Together, they have always found some of God's greatest blessings while riding horses and enjoying His creation.

Answering God's call

When God opened the door for this ministry, Bruce and Heather answered His call. Today, they are honored to serve Maple Valley and the surrounding communities.

Their heart is to share the hope, love, and truth of Jesus Christ with anyone who walks through the doors.

WHAT MATTERS HERE

Faith, family, and a welcome that feels like home.

Bible-based faith

We grow together in God's Word and seek to keep Christ at the center.

Come-as-you-are welcome

Whether you have attended church your whole life or are simply curious, there is a place for you here.

Encouraging families

Sunday school and family encouragement are part of the heart of this ministry.

Serving Maple Valley

We are honored to serve Coral, Maple Valley, and the surrounding communities.
