Our Story

Your Perfect Day Awaits

Nestled in the picturesque landscape of Maple Valley, Dandelion Chapel offers a unique wedding experience that blends the warmth of cowboy tradition with the sacred beauty of a countryside chapel.

Our venue celebrates the spirit of the West while providing all the comforts and elegance your special day deserves. More than a wedding venue — we are a living, breathing church community rooted in faith, family, and the open sky.
